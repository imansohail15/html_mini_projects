Biryani Recipe

Dish Description:

Biryani is a celebrated South Asian mixed rice dish made by layering perfectly cooked Basmati rice with a rich, aromatic meat or vegetable curry. The key to an authentic biryani lies in parboiling the long-grain rice with whole spices like cardamom and cloves, then layering it over a base of tender marinated meat (such as chicken, mutton, or beef) or vegetables cooked with yogurt, garlic, ginger, and a blend of spices. The dish is sealed and slow-cooked on low heat—a technique known as dum—allowing the rice to steam in the rich flavors of the curry.

Ingredients:

Main Base Ingredients

Items Quantity
Chicken 1.5 kg
Rice 1 kg
Onions 3-4(large)
Tomatoes 3-4(large)
Ginger-Garlic Paste 2 tablespoons
Green Chillies 6-8(chopped)

Cooking Oil And Ghee

Cooking oil 100/200 ml

Spices and Seasoning

Biryani Masala Powder 2-3 tablespoons
Red Chilli powder 1-2 tablespoons
Turmeric powder 1/2 to 1 teaspoon
Salt 2-3 tablespoons

Whole Spices (for Rice & Gravy)

Bay leaves 2-3 pieces
Green cardamom 1 pod
Black cardamom 1 pod

Instruction Section:

  • Marinate the Meat: Combine chicken or mutton with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, red chili powder, turmeric, biryani masala, salt, and lemon juice; let it marinate for at least 30 minutes to overnight.
  • Fry the Onions: Slice onions thinly and fry them in hot oil or ghee until golden and crisp, then set half of them aside for layering.
  • Cook the Meat Gravy (Masala): In a heavy-bottomed pot, temper whole spices, sauté ginger-garlic paste and tomatoes, add the marinated meat and half the fried onions, and simmer until the meat is tender and a thick gravy forms.
  • Boil the Rice: In a separate large pot, boil water with whole spices and plenty of salt; add soaked basmati rice and cook until it is about 70% to 80% done (firm in the center), then drain immediately.
  • Layer the Biryani: In a large heavy pot, alternate layers of the cooked meat gravy and the par-boiled rice, sprinkling chopped mint, cilantro, saffron-infused milk, and the remaining crispy fried onions between the layers.
  • Dum Cooking (Steam): Cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid or seal it with foil/dough, cook on high heat for a few minutes, then lower the heat to a minimum and let it steam for 15 to 30 minutes.
  • Rest and Serve: Turn off the heat, let the biryani rest undisturbed for 15 to 30 minutes, then gently fluff and mix the layers from the side before serving.
